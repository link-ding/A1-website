Our approach

Confidence with unfamiliar questions

The OC test asks capable primary students to reason quickly across unfamiliar material. Effective preparation builds the underlying literacy and numeracy first, then adds strategies for pace, elimination and checking.

Our tutors keep the work age-appropriate and purposeful. Each mistake becomes information: perhaps a vocabulary gap, an overlooked condition or a strategy that breaks down under time. Teaching responds to that cause.

Common questions

Before you enquire.

What subjects are in OC preparation?

The NSW OC placement test focuses on reading, mathematical reasoning and thinking skills. Preparation is shaped around those domains.

Is OC tutoring suitable for Year 4 students?

OC preparation commonly takes place in Year 4 for entry to Year 5, but the right timing depends on the student and the current NSW process.

Do you guarantee an OC placement?

No ethical tutor can guarantee placement. We focus on stronger skills, better test habits and preparation that reflects the student’s actual needs.
