Our approach

Use the assessment as a diagnostic

NAPLAN can reveal where a student’s literacy or numeracy foundation needs attention. Preparation should respond to that evidence, not become months of repetitive test papers.

Academy One tutors identify the skill beneath each error, teach it clearly and then return to exam-style questions so students can apply it independently. Familiarity with the online format and timing is added without letting the test dominate learning.

What we cover

Support matched to the next learning goal.

Start with the student’s course, current work and assessment priorities.

Reading

Locate evidence, infer meaning and understand how texts work.

Writing

Plan, sequence and control language for the given purpose.

Language conventions

Apply grammar, punctuation and spelling in context.

Numeracy

Reason with number, measurement, space, statistics and probability.

How lessons work

A simple cycle that keeps moving.

  1. 01

    Diagnose

    Group errors by the underlying skill, not just the question.

  2. 02

    Teach

    Use short, explicit explanations and worked examples.

  3. 03

    Apply

    Return to NAPLAN-style items with decreasing support.

  4. 04

    Transfer

    Connect the improvement to everyday schoolwork.

Common questions

Before you enquire.

Which year levels do you support for NAPLAN?

NAPLAN is sat in Years 3, 5, 7 and 9. Tutor availability and the recommended format depend on the student’s age and needs.

Will tutoring only use practice papers?

No. Practice questions help with familiarity, but lessons focus on the literacy and numeracy skills that produce lasting improvement.

Can you focus on just writing or numeracy?

Yes. A targeted program can focus on one domain when school data or a student’s work shows a clear need.
