Our approach

From formula recall to physical reasoning

Physics questions change their surface details, but the underlying models remain consistent. Strong students can identify the system, choose a useful representation and explain what the mathematics means. That is the habit our tutors develop.

We work through diagrams, assumptions, units and written justification alongside calculations. Students learn how to approach unfamiliar questions calmly and how to present a solution that a marker can follow.

How lessons work

A simple cycle that keeps moving.

  1. 01

    Model

    Translate a situation into forces, fields, motion or energy.

  2. 02

    Represent

    Use diagrams, graphs and equations to organise reasoning.

  3. 03

    Solve

    Work accurately while checking units and plausibility.

  4. 04

    Explain

    Communicate cause and effect in precise scientific language.

Common questions

Before you enquire.

Which Physics modules can you help with?

Tutoring can cover the full Year 11 and HSC Physics course, from mechanics and waves to electromagnetism and the nature of light and matter.

Is the tutoring suitable for advanced students?

Yes. Lessons can consolidate fundamentals or extend students through unfamiliar and multi-step problems.

Can Physics and Maths tutoring be coordinated?

Where useful, we can consider both subjects when matching a tutor and planning support, particularly for algebra, graphs and calculus-based reasoning.
