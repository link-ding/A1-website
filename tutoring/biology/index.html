Our approach

See the relationships behind the content

Biology has a large vocabulary, but recall alone is not enough. Students need to explain processes, interpret evidence and connect changes at molecular, organism and population levels. We organise the course around those relationships.

Tutors combine active recall with diagrams, data questions and extended responses. The aim is a study system that remains useful after the lesson, especially when students face cumulative exams.

Common questions

Before you enquire.

Do you cover all HSC Biology modules?

Yes. Support can cover heredity, genetic change, infectious disease and non-infectious disease, as well as Year 11 foundations.

Can tutors help improve long responses?

Yes. Students practise selecting relevant evidence, sequencing cause and effect, and matching response length to the marks available.

Is tutoring individual or in groups?

Academy One offers one-to-one tutoring and selected small groups. Availability depends on year, subject and timetable.
