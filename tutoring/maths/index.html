Our approach

A clearer way through maths

Strong maths results do not come from collecting more formulas. They come from understanding why a method works, recognising when to use it and practising it with feedback. Academy One tutors identify the specific gaps slowing a student down, then build a sequence that connects schoolwork, fundamentals and assessment goals.

Lessons can support current class content, repair earlier gaps or extend capable students into harder problems. The format stays responsive: one-to-one tuition for precise support, or small groups for structured teaching and shared momentum.

Common questions

Before you enquire.

Which maths courses do you cover?

Academy One supports primary and high school maths, including Mathematics Standard, Advanced, Extension 1 and Extension 2. Tutor availability varies, so contact us with the student’s year and course.

Can tutoring follow the student’s school program?

Yes. Lessons can align with the student’s current school topic and upcoming assessments while still addressing prerequisite gaps.

Do you offer online maths tutoring?

Yes. Students can learn in person in Chatswood or online, depending on tutor availability and the learning format that suits them.
