Our approach

A real teaching space in the centre of Chatswood

Academy One is based at Suite 403, 815 Pacific Highway, Chatswood NSW 2067. Students learn in a focused environment that is convenient for families travelling through Chatswood and across Sydney’s North Shore.

Being local makes regular tuition easier to sustain. Families can combine lessons with the school commute, and students can build a consistent routine around the same tutor, place and weekly goals.
