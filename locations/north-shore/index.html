Our approach

Specialist support within reach

Families come to Academy One from Chatswood, Artarmon, Willoughby, Lane Cove, Roseville, Lindfield, Killara, Gordon and Pymble. Our Chatswood base offers a central place for consistent, subject-specific tuition.

The goal is not to add more busywork to an already full week. Tutors focus on the concepts, feedback and exam habits that will make the greatest difference, then set clear priorities between lessons.

Common questions

Before you enquire.

Do you have centres in every North Shore suburb?

Academy One’s physical centre is in Chatswood. We serve students from surrounding North Shore suburbs in person and may offer online lessons.

Which North Shore schools do your students attend?

Academy One has taught students from a range of selective, independent and comprehensive schools across the area. The teaching is matched to the student’s course and needs rather than a single school program.

Can siblings study different subjects at the centre?

Potentially, depending on tutor and timetable availability. Include both students’ subjects, years and preferred times in your enquiry.
